# REQUIREMENT TRACEABILITY MATRIX (RTM)
## Stockledger / FactoryFlow ERP
**Enterprise Food Manufacturing Order-to-Audit Management System**

| Req ID | Module Name | Business Requirement & Acceptance Criteria | Architectural Component | Database Tables | Automated Test Verification |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **REQ-A** | Authentication & User Management | Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Admin, Production Supervisor, Sales Executive, Inventory Manager, Financial Auditor. JWT auth, refresh token rotation, bcrypt password hashing. | `auth.service.ts`, `rbac.middleware.ts` | `users`, `roles`, `permissions`, `user_sessions`, `audit_logs` | `auth.test.ts` (Login, invalid auth, token expiry, privilege escalation) |
| **REQ-B** | Customer Management | B2B distributor profiles, GSTIN verification, credit limits, payment terms, delivery addresses, state/tax zone (Intra vs Inter-state). | `customer.service.ts`, `customer.controller.ts` | `customers`, `customer_addresses`, `credit_profiles` | `customer.test.ts` (State detection, GSTIN format, credit limit enforcement) |
| **REQ-C** | Supplier Management | Vendor records, GSTIN, raw material catalog, payment terms, performance rating, lead times. | `supplier.service.ts` | `suppliers`, `supplier_materials` | `supplier.test.ts` (Vendor creation, linking materials) |
| **REQ-D** | Product Catalogue | Finished Goods master (e.g., Murukku 200g), HSN codes (e.g., 1905), standard packaging sizes, selling price (₹50), GST rate (5%). | `product.service.ts` | `products`, `product_categories`, `uom_conversions` | `product.test.ts` (HSN validation, UOM mapping) |
| **REQ-E** | Raw Material Management | Ingredients inventory (Flour, Oil, Spice mix). UOMs (kg, Litre, g). Min/Max thresholds, reorder alerts. | `material.service.ts` | `materials`, `material_categories`, `stock_ledger` | `material.test.ts` (Decimal quantity precision, UOM conversion) |
| **REQ-F** | Packaging Material Management | Packaging inventory (Pouches, Cartons). Piece-based counting, scrap/damage tracking. | `packaging.service.ts` | `materials` (category=PACKAGING), `stock_ledger` | `packaging.test.ts` (Damaged pouches scrap accounting) |
| **REQ-G** | Bill of Materials (BOM) Management | Version-controlled recipe definitions. Standard batch yield (e.g., 200 packets for Murukku 200g). Exact material requirements. | `bom.service.ts`, `bom.validator.ts` | `boms`, `bom_items`, `bom_versions` | `bom.test.ts` (Version immutability, active BOM resolution) |
| **REQ-H** | Purchase Management | PO creation, approval, Goods Receipt Note (GRN), invoice matching, batch lot assignment, weighted-average rate recalculation. | `purchase.service.ts`, `grn.service.ts` | `purchase_orders`, `purchase_order_items`, `grn_receipts`, `stock_ledger` | `purchase.test.ts` (Step 1: 60kg flour purchase @ ₹45/kg, weighted average rate calculation) |
| **REQ-I** | Sales Order Management | Salesman submits customer order. Real-time availability check, backorder handling, reserved stock tracking. | `order.service.ts`, `stock_availability.service.ts` | `sales_orders`, `sales_order_items` | `sales_order.test.ts` (Order submission, multi-salesman concurrent validation) |
| **REQ-J** | Order Approval | Admin authorization workflow. Credit limit checks, stock reservation lock, approval audit stamp. | `approval.service.ts` | `sales_orders`, `order_approvals`, `audit_logs` | `order_approval.test.ts` (Approval state transitions, stock reservation) |
| **REQ-K** | GST Invoice Management | Statutory compliance: Taxable value, CGST (2.5%), SGST (2.5%), IGST (5%). Sequential invoice numbering, HSN breakdown, immutability upon posting. | `invoice.service.ts`, `gst_calculator.service.ts` | `invoices`, `invoice_items`, `tax_ledgers` | `invoice.test.ts` (Step 9: ₹7,500 taxable, ₹187.50 CGST, ₹187.50 SGST, ₹7,875 total) |
| **REQ-L** | Dispatch Management | Delivery challan, physical warehouse stock decrement, stock ledger entry posting, vehicle/e-way bill details. | `dispatch.service.ts` | `dispatches`, `dispatch_items`, `stock_ledger` | `dispatch.test.ts` (Step 9: 150 packets dispatched, FG balance 238 -> 88 packets) |
| **REQ-M** | Production Management | Batch creation, active BOM locking, actual material issue (Flour, Oil, Spices, Pouches, Cartons), good output recording, scrap recording. | `production.service.ts`, `material_issue.service.ts` | `production_batches`, `batch_material_issues`, `batch_outputs`, `stock_ledger` | `production.test.ts` (Atomic batch completion, material deduction, FG addition) |
| **REQ-N** | Inventory Ledger (Double-Entry) | Immutable ledger: Item ID, txn type, quantity change (+/-), unit, reference doc, user ID, timestamp. Strict negative balance prohibition. | `stock_ledger.service.ts` | `stock_ledger`, `stock_balances` | `ledger.test.ts` (Complete ledger reconciliation, concurrency lock, auditability) |
| **REQ-O** | Production Variance Analysis | Planned vs actual consumption. Yield variance calculation (e.g., -6%), ingredient variance (e.g., +5% oil), cost impact of variance. | `variance.service.ts` | `production_variances`, `production_batches` | `variance.test.ts` (Step 5 & 3: Yield variance -6%, Oil variance +5%) |
| **REQ-P** | Product Costing Engine | Total batch cost = Raw materials (weighted avg) + Packaging + Direct Labour (₹1,200) + Power/Gas (₹600). Cost per good packet, gross margin analysis. | `costing.service.ts`, `valuation.service.ts` | `batch_costing`, `cost_allocations` | `costing.test.ts` (Step 13-14: Batch cost ₹5,855.00, ₹31.14/packet, 37.7% margin) |
| **REQ-Q** | Payment Management | Receipts from distributors, partial payments, multiple modes (NEFT, UPI, Cheque), ledger posting. | `payment.service.ts` | `payments`, `payment_allocations`, `invoices` | `payment.test.ts` (Step 12: ₹4,000 partial payment recording) |
| **REQ-R** | Receivables & Ageing | Outstanding calculation (₹7,875 - ₹4,000 = ₹3,875), ageing buckets (0-30, 31-60, 61-90, 90+ days), credit risk flags. | `receivables.service.ts` | `customer_ledgers`, `invoices` | `receivables.test.ts` (Step 12: ₹3,875 balance outstanding, ageing bucket allocation) |
| **REQ-S** | Reports and Auditing | Traceable audit trail: Material traceability from supplier GRN to Batch to Invoice. Statutory GST summary, stock valuation report. | `report.service.ts`, `audit.service.ts` | `audit_logs`, materialized views | `audit.test.ts` (Forward/backward lot trace, ledger checksum verification) |
| **REQ-T** | Owner Dashboard & Executive Insights | High-level KPIs: Real-time inventory valuation, daily production yield, revenue, gross margin, receivables exposure, stockout warnings. | `dashboard.service.ts` | Cached analytics queries / Aggregations | `dashboard.test.ts` (Real-time aggregation accuracy without mock data) |
