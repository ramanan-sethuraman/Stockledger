# RISK REGISTER & MITIGATION STRATEGY
## FactoryFlow ERP / Stockledger

| Risk ID | Risk Category | Risk Description | Severity | Probability | Impact Analysis | Mitigation Strategy & Architectural Safeguard |
|:---|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|:---|:---|
| **RSK-01** | Financial Integrity | Floating-point rounding error in monetary calculations (e.g. `0.1 + 0.2 !== 0.3`). | **CRITICAL** | High | Penny discrepancy in GST reports or invoice totals causes audit failure and legal non-compliance. | Use Prisma `Decimal` / `decimal.js` / integer paisa arithmetic throughout the entire backend. Never perform raw JS float arithmetic on currency or quantities. |
| **RSK-02** | Inventory Integrity | Race conditions during concurrent sales orders or production dispatches causing negative stock balances. | **CRITICAL** | Medium | Overselling product or consuming materials that do not exist physically in the factory. | Enforce database transactions with optimistic version locking on balances and strict check constraint `CHECK (balance >= 0)`. Atomic rollback on insufficient stock. |
| **RSK-03** | Data Consistency | Partial failure during production batch completion (e.g. raw materials deducted, but server crashes before finished goods credited). | **CRITICAL** | Medium | Ghost stock consumption; irreversible discrepancy between physical warehouse and digital ledger. | Wrap batch completion in an ACID transaction boundary (`prisma.$transaction`). All 6 material deductions + FG credit + variance + costing commit atomically or all rollback. |
| **RSK-04** | Statutory Compliance | Inaccurate GST split (e.g. charging IGST instead of CGST/SGST on intra-state Tamil Nadu transactions). | **HIGH** | Medium | Tax liability miscalculation; invalid tax invoices under Indian GST Act. | Automated state-code matching: If `Supplier.state_code == Customer.state_code` (e.g. "33" for Tamil Nadu), force CGST (2.5%) + SGST (2.5%). Else apply IGST (5.0%). |
| **RSK-05** | Production Variance | Inaccurate reporting of damaged packaging or scrap leading to skewed cost per good packet. | **HIGH** | Medium | Underestimating manufacturing cost; setting unviable wholesale selling prices. | Separate fields for `good_output_qty` vs `rejected_qty`. Damaged pouches absorbed into batch total cost so unit cost reflects real yield: `Total Cost / Good Units`. |
| **RSK-06** | Valuation Method Drift| Inconsistent material valuation method (mixing FIFO and Weighted-Average). | **HIGH** | Low | Inaccurate COGS (Cost of Goods Sold) and distorted gross margin analytics. | Enforce Perpetual Moving Weighted Average Costing engine: On every GRN receipt, compute `new_avg = ((old_qty * old_rate) + (new_qty * new_rate)) / total_qty`. |
| **RSK-07** | Security & Auth | Privilege escalation (e.g. a Salesman calling Admin approval endpoint or modifying unit prices). | **HIGH** | Medium | Unauthorized order approvals or fraud. | Strict server-side RBAC middleware (`requireRole(['ADMIN'])`) on every sensitive mutation. Front-end UI permissions are visual only; backend is the authoritative barrier. |
| **RSK-08** | Audit Invalidation | Accidental deletion or mutation of historical stock ledger or posted invoice records. | **CRITICAL** | Low | Auditor cannot verify financial history; violation of fundamental ERP accounting rules. | Immutable ledger design: Stock ledger table has no `UPDATE` or `DELETE` endpoints. Voiding an invoice requires an explicit Credit Note transaction. |
